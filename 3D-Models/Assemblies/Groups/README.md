# Assembly navigation groups

Grouping pages below identify **where assembly records belong functionally**. An actual registered assembly has an ID-stable home under `3D-Models/Assemblies/SS-NNN/` and can be linked from multiple groups.

- [Integrated rig](Integrated-Rig/README.md): Full-system relationships: verified mechanical group positioning, overall clearances, stage-to-chamber orientation and build manifests.
- [Chamber and port interface](Chamber-and-Port-Interface/README.md): Subject-facing enclosure with two access ports, door mechanisms, spout alignment and clearance checks.
- [Stimulus storage and selection](Stimulus-Storage-and-Selection/README.md): Reservoir/bottle modules, carriage and mechanical selection interfaces; current configuration has two selectable experimental positions and HOME.
- [Stimulus delivery and fluid path](Delivery-and-Fluid-Path/README.md): Physical connections from reservoir outlets to stimulus presentation, including confirmed valves and services.
- [Actuation and sensing](Actuation-and-Sensing/README.md): Port-door actuators, motor positioning hardware and mounted sensors; software/logic remains separate from physical models.
- [Utilities and maintenance](Utilities-and-Maintenance/README.md): Conditional assemblies for pressure support, rinse/vacuum, waste management and servicing.

Register included component IDs, exact revisions, quantities, mating interfaces and required CAD reference tests. A build configuration specifies which assembly revisions have actually been installed or tested.
