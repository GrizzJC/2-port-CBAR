# Animal chamber and access face

## Scope

Chamber shells, animal-facing panels, removable lids, trays, approach openings, alignment guides and brackets that establish animal-facing geometry.

## Interfaces

Port shutters and guides, spout mounts, position sensors and frame.

## Placement and validation considerations

Check clearances, removable access, port-to-spout geometry and cleaning. A previous 3-port chamber is not assumed to fit.

## Registered parts and assemblies

No asset links are entered until a source model and its relation to the two-port rig have been verified.

## Adding a model

Register its original filename, editable source, source format, proposed stable asset ID, dependencies, functional and assembly relationships, source revision, manufacturing outputs and verification in the [3D asset tracker](../../../tracking/README.md). Store physical design files at their controlled [component](../../Components/README.md) or [assembly](../../Assemblies/README.md) location rather than duplicating files in this functional index.
